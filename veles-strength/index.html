Upright section

FAQ

Freight, warranty,
straight answers.

The questions the trade desk answers every day. Anything else — call, we pick up.

How much is freight, and how long does it take?

Melbourne metro is free over $500 and typically lands in 48 hours. Nationally we palletise and ship tail-lift to the door: Sydney/Adelaide 3–4 business days, Brisbane 4–5, Perth 6–8. Freight is free on orders over $1,500, otherwise quoted at checkout by postcode and dead weight.

What does the 10-year warranty actually cover?

Structural steel — uprights, crossmembers, base — against failure or permanent deformation in normal use, transferable to a new owner. Moving parts, pads and upholstery carry two years, bar shafts are lifetime against bending under rated load. Finish wear, chalk and rust from outdoor storage aren't covered, and we'll say so up front.

Can I return something if I got it wrong?

Thirty-day change of mind on anything still boxed and unmarked — you cover return freight, we refund in full. Opened but unused, we'll usually take it back with a 15% restock. Custom powder-coat and OEM runs are made to order, so they're quote-final.

Do you install, and where?

Our own crews cover Victoria, and we run partner crews in NSW and QLD. Rigs, platforms and flooring are the usual scope — a studio takes a day, a full commercial room one to three. Install is quoted with the CAD layout so there are no surprises on the day.

Who qualifies for trade pricing?

Any business with an ABN that trains people or sells equipment: gyms, studios, PTs, physios, schools, clubs and resellers. Apply with your ABN and a website or social account; approvals usually come back within one business day. Home-gym buyers get retail — which is still built on a wholesaler's margin.

Is what's on the site actually in stock?

If it says in stock, it's on a shelf in Maribyrnong — the site counts the same inventory the forklift does. Out-of-stock lines show the next container's ETA, and you can reserve against it with a 10% deposit that's refundable until the day it lands.
