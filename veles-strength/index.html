The Steel

The boring numbers
are the whole point.

Anyone can powder-coat a rack black. Here's what we actually argue about with the factory.

75×75mm

Upright section

11-gauge, 3 mm wall. Not the 50 × 50 mm 2 mm you'll find on a $400 rack that wobbles at 140 kg.

1,000kg

Static load tested

Every frame, not every batch. Test sheet ships in the box with the serial number stamped on the base plate.

215kPSI

Bar tensile strength

Japanese steel shaft, bronze bushings, 190 mm loadable sleeve. Whip where you want it, none where you don't.

±10g

Plate tolerance

Calibrated range weighed individually and drift-checked. Your 180 is a real 180 on both ends of the bar.

10yr

Frame warranty

Structural, transferable, no gymnastics. Two years on moving parts and pads, lifetime on the bar shaft.

48hr

Dispatch, in stock

Held in Maribyrnong. If it says in stock, it's twenty metres from a forklift, not eight weeks from Ningbo.
